I came up through data-center operations — including IBM mainframe work — before moving to network security and blue team engineering. I hold a B.S. in Cybersecurity and most of my hands-on time these days is split between building lab environments, drilling certification material, and writing up what I've learned.

The site exists to do three things: keep a public record of the projects, document walkthroughs other people might find useful, and force me to write clearly about what I'm doing. The third one is the most important.
